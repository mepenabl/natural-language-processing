# Lab 4 - Transformer Models

## Overview

This laboratory explored transformer-based architectures and their applications in modern Natural Language Processing tasks.

The project focused on understanding how transformer models process contextual information and generate contextualized representations for language understanding and generation.

The implementation was developed using:
- HuggingFace Transformers
- PyTorch
- NumPy
- Pandas
- Matplotlib

---

## Objectives

The main goals of the laboratory were:

1. Understand transformer architectures
2. Explore self-attention mechanisms
3. Work with pretrained transformer models
4. Generate contextual embeddings
5. Analyse token-level representations
6. Study contextual language understanding

---

## Transformer Architectures

The laboratory introduced the main concepts behind transformer models, including:

- self-attention
- contextual embeddings
- sequence encoding
- tokenization
- positional representations
- pretrained language models

Unlike static embeddings, transformer models generate contextualized representations where the meaning of a word depends on the surrounding context.

---

## Contextual Embeddings

Several experiments were performed to analyse contextual representations generated by transformer models.

The workflow included:
- loading pretrained models
- tokenizing sentences
- generating embeddings
- analysing contextual outputs
- studying semantic behaviour

The experiments demonstrated how contextual embeddings adapt dynamically depending on sentence structure and meaning.

---

## Attention Mechanisms

Special attention was given to the role of self-attention within transformer architectures.

The experiments highlighted how attention mechanisms allow models to:
- capture long-range dependencies
- model contextual relationships
- improve semantic understanding
- process sequential information efficiently

---

## Advantages over Classical NLP Approaches

The laboratory compared transformer models with traditional NLP techniques such as:
- Bag-of-Words
- TF-IDF
- static word embeddings
- classical machine learning pipelines

The experiments showed that transformers provide:
- richer semantic representations
- contextual awareness
- improved flexibility
- stronger language understanding capabilities

---

## Observations

Several important observations emerged during experimentation:

- transformer models capture contextual semantics effectively
- pretrained models reduce training requirements
- contextual embeddings improve representation quality
- tokenization strategies impact performance
- attention mechanisms improve sequence modelling

---

## Conclusions

The laboratory demonstrated why transformer architectures represent the current state-of-the-art paradigm in Natural Language Processing.

The project provided practical experience with:
- transformer inference
- contextual embeddings
- pretrained language models
- token-level analysis
- semantic representation learning

The experiments also highlighted the importance of:
- contextual reasoning
- efficient tokenization
- pretrained model selection
- semantic understanding in NLP systems
