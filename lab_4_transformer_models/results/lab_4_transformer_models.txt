LAB 4 — TRANSFORMER MODELS

This laboratory focused on transformer-based Natural Language Processing models and their applications in modern NLP tasks.

The project explored how transformer architectures process contextual information and generate contextualized vector representations for language understanding tasks.

The implementation was developed using:
- HuggingFace Transformers
- PyTorch
- NumPy
- Pandas
- Matplotlib

The laboratory introduced the fundamental concepts behind transformer architectures, including:
- self-attention mechanisms
- contextual embeddings
- tokenization strategies
- sequence modeling
- pretrained language models

Several experiments were performed using transformer-based models for text processing and representation learning.

The workflow included:
- loading pretrained transformer models
- tokenizing textual inputs
- generating contextual embeddings
- analysing token-level representations
- evaluating transformer outputs

The experiments demonstrated how transformer models differ from static embeddings by producing contextualized representations, where the meaning of a word changes depending on the surrounding sentence context.

The laboratory also explored:
- sequence encoding
- probability distributions over tokens
- contextual inference
- semantic understanding

Special attention was given to the advantages of transformer architectures over traditional NLP approaches such as:
- Bag-of-Words models
- static embeddings
- classical machine learning pipelines

The experiments highlighted several important transformer properties:

- ability to capture long-range dependencies
- contextual understanding
- semantic flexibility
- improved language modelling capabilities

The project additionally emphasized the importance of:
- tokenization quality
- model selection
- preprocessing
- inference efficiency

Several observations were made during experimentation:

- transformer models produce highly contextual representations
- attention mechanisms improve semantic understanding
- pretrained models significantly reduce training requirements
- contextual embeddings outperform static representations in complex NLP tasks

The laboratory provided practical experience with:
- transformer architectures
- contextual language models
- HuggingFace Transformers
- token embeddings
- sequence modelling
- NLP inference pipelines
- contextual semantic analysis

Overall, the project demonstrated how transformer-based models represent the current state-of-the-art paradigm in Natural Language Processing and how they can be applied to advanced language understanding and generation tasks.
